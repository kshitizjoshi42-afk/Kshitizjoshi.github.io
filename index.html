CURRENT RESEARCH

Climate Variability and Agricultural Production in the Marsyangdi River Basin, Nepal

⌖ Lamjung, Nepal

This project investigates how changing temperature, precipitation and hydrological variability are associated with changes in crop production and agricultural calendars in vulnerable communities of Besisahar Municipality and Marsyangdi Rural Municipality.

The study focuses primarily on rice, maize and seasonal vegetables and combines secondary agricultural production and yield records with localized climate data and field research.

Research focus

  • Climate trends and variability
  • Crop production and yield dynamics
  • Climate–yield statistical relationships
  • Changing agricultural calendars
  • Farmer vulnerability and adaptation

Methodology

Historical crop production and yield data will be reviewed alongside temperature and precipitation records. Trend, correlation and appropriate regression-based analyses will be used to assess whether variations in climatic conditions are associated with changes in crop yield. Surveys, focus group discussions and key informant interviews will complement the quantitative analysis by documenting farmer experiences, perceived climate impacts and local adaptation strategies.

WHY FIELD RESEARCH?

Climate records can show how environmental conditions are changing, while agricultural statistics can reveal production trends. Field research adds the human dimension by showing how those changes are experienced, understood and addressed by communities.
